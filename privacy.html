Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-14

Who we are

"AI Video Publisher" is a private tool operated by the owner of the Realized Politics YouTube channel. Contact: realizedpolitics@gmail.com.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's explicit consent, the app uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API for the owner's own channel only: to upload videos, change their visibility, and read the channel's own video information and analytics.

How data is used and stored

OAuth tokens and channel data are stored only on computers controlled by the channel owner. They are used solely to operate the owner's own channel. We do not sell, share, or transfer this data to anyone, we do not use it for advertising, and no person reads it other than the channel owner.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Visitors

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or forms and does not collect information about visitors.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored tokens are deleted by the owner on request or when access is revoked.
